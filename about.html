About

Hi I'm Jo! I'm a Nottingham based creative specialising in print and digital design, with a M.A. in Design and B.A. Interactive Design from the University of Lincoln.

I am currently experiencing peak whimsy in my work life. I currently work at Blind Box Empire, selling specially curated blind boxes from around the world. I've also been selling my art at markets.

My ultimate career goal is to sink myself in a full time design role that can either utilise my technical skills, or around my primary interests (beauty and healthcare, music and travel).

FAQ

Why did you leave your role at We Print Lanyards to work in retail?
My mum passed away really suddenly of cancer. I tried to get back life back as usual, but the grief, extra responsibilities both in and out of work was too much.
